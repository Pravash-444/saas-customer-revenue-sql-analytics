# Raw Data

This folder contains the original synthetic CSV datasets used in the SaaS Customer & Revenue Analytics project.

The datasets represent the existing PostgreSQL tables used for customer, subscription, payment, plan, and product usage analysis.

## Files

- `customers.csv` — Customer details, signup dates, countries, and acquisition channels.
- `plans.csv` — Subscription plan details, pricing, and billing cycles.
- `subscriptions.csv` — Customer subscription records, start dates, end dates, and statuses.
- `payments.csv` — Payment transactions, amounts, statuses, and payment methods.
- `usage_events.csv` — Customer product usage events and duration details.

## Notes

- The datasets are synthetic and intended for learning and portfolio demonstration.
- These files represent the raw data used in the analysis.
- No real customer or confidential business information is included.
